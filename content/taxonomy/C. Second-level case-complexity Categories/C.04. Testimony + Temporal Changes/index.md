{{< sc >}}Testimony{{< /sc >}} subsumes [case-types] in which S gains a true belief – e.g., [Successful Testimony]{{< fn 1 "This also applies for [Correct Reasoning From False Testimony], [Testimony-Based Successful Implication], [Unbelieving Attester], [Silent Approval of Correct Testimony], [Irrational Attester], [Unbelieving Attester] and [Correct Reasoning from an Unreliable Attester’s Testimony]. In my exemplary [case-types] below I use [Successful Testimony], because it’s plausible to assume that [Successful Testimony] is associated with a strong and stable knowledge-intuition. Whether tokens of those other [types] evoke the same uniform evaluation-behaviour requires further research. At present, such data is lacking. I deprioritise combining variations of these [case types] with {{< sc >}}Temporal Changes{{< /sc >}} accordingly." >}} – as well as ones where S forms a false belief – [False Testimony], [Testimony-Based False Implication], [Unsuccessful Transmission]. The elementary {{< sc >}}Category Temporal Changes{{< /sc >}} comprises four kinds of change; all of which can be complicated by Testimony. Below I detail and illustrate what I believe to be the most relevant and obvious combinations. The overview is not exhaustive.
