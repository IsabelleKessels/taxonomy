id: C.7
title: Truth From False Premise + Counterfactual-Interference
---

In {{< sc >}}Counterfactual-Interference{{< /sc >}} cases S gains a true belief based on _prima facie_ suitable evidence in the @-world, but there would be an interference in modally close possible worlds such that S would gain a false belief. By itself, the modal profile of {{< sc >}}Counterfactual-Interference{{< /sc >}} cases is such that S gains a true belief based on _prima facie_ suitable evidence in the @-world – and no misleading evidence is present in their environment. In modally close possible worlds, however, S would gain a false belief based on misleading evidence because of the interference. The complicating factor of {{< sc >}}Truth From False Premise{{< /sc >}} is characterised by S encountering misleading evidence on which they (explicitly or implicitly) form a false belief q, from which they infer a belief p made accurate by some backup truth-maker. By itself, the modal profile of the ‘standard Gettier’ variety of {{< sc >}}Truth From False Premise{{< /sc >}} cases is such that the _gettiering_ (i.e., the presence of misleading evidence and an _independent_ backup truth-maker) happens in the @-world, but S would gain a false belief that p in modally close possible worlds wherein the backup truth-maker wouldn’t be present, but the misleading evidence for q would be. The modal profile of the ‘Knowledge From Falsehood’ variety is such, that the misleading evidence present the @-world is interdependent on the presence of the backup truth-maker, so that there are no close possible worlds in which the backup truth-maker wouldn’t be present, but the misleading evidence would be.

In combining these {{< sc >}}Categories{{< /sc >}}, I presume, firstly, that {{< sc >}}Truth From False Premise{{< /sc >}} requires the combined presence of misleading evidence and a backup truth-maker, and, secondly, that a {{< sc >}}Counterfactual-Interference{{< /sc >}} minimally involves an interference that would occur in modally close possible worlds, but not in the case’s @-world. There are three ways to merge this:

- The gettiering _occurs_ in the @-world where no interference takes place, but an interference would prevent the gettiering in modally close possible worlds.
- The gettiering _would occur_ in the modally close possible worlds due to an interference, which doesn’t take place in the @-world.
- The gettiering _occurs_ in the @-world where no interference takes place, _and_ in modally close possible worlds where an interference would take place.

Note that I chose to speak of ‘gettiering’ specifically, rather than ‘believing a truth on a false premise’. The question whether the relationship between the presence of a backup truth-maker and misleading evidence is or isn’t independent becomes difficult to answer in these scenarios.
