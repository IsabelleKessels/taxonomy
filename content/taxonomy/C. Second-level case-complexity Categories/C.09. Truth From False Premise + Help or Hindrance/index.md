---
id: C.9
title: Truth From False Premise + Help or Hindrance
---

In {{< sc >}}Truth From False Premise{{< /sc >}} cases, S encounters misleading evidence on which they (explicitly or implicitly) form a false belief q, from which they infer a belief p made accurate by some backup truth-maker.{{< fn 1 "In this preliminary taxonomy, I build separate [types] in this complex {{< sc >}}Category{{< /sc >}} based on whether the _gettiering_ manifests as [Direct Gettier] or [False Premise Gettier], because they differ in whether they’re combinable with [Direct Cognitive Hindrance] and [Indirect Cognitive Hindrance]. I don’t, however, build separate [case-types] in which the _gettiering_ manifests as [Instrument Gettier]. Such [case-types] can (and should) be established _if_ x-phi results on tokens of the corresponding elementary [type] indicate different evaluation-behaviours as compared to [Direct Gettier] or [False Premise Gettier]. Same applies for [Knowledge From Falsehood] and [Instrument Knowledge From Falsehood]." >}} The most obvious combination with {{< sc >}}Help or Hindrance{{< /sc >}} is [case-types] where _hindrance_ leads S to form the false belief that q, or to misperceive the situation. [Direct Cognitive Hindrance] and [Indirect Cognitive Hindrance] target the _belief-formation process_, and are therefore only combinable with [False Premise Gettier] where a false belief that q is _consciously and explicitly_ held by S. When the hindrance causing S to gain a false belief is that something that influences S’s belief-formation process, it’s reasonable to assume that the presence of this hindrance isn’t interdependent on the presence of a backup truth-maker, so I’d say this is not combinable with [Knowledge From Falsehood].

For [Sensory Hindrance], _gettiering_ can take the form of [Direct Gettier] or [False Premise Gettier]; depending on how widely one frames ‘interferences with the perceptual process,’ a version featuring [Knowledge From Falsehood] can be constructed as well.

Beyond this, [case-types] can be build where S forms the false belief that q or misperceives something in the situation due to [Insufficient Indirect Cognitive Help] or [Insufficient Sensory Help].
