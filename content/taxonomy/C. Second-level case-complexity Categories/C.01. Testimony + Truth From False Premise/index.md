Truth From False Premise covers _gettiering_{{< fn 1 "In this preliminary taxonomy, I don’t differentiate [case-types] in this complex {{< sc >}}Category{{< /sc >}} by whether the _gettering_ manifest as [Direct Gettier], [False Premise Gettier] or [Instrument Gettier]. Separate [case-types] can (and should) be established _if_ x-phi results on tokens of the corresponding elementary [types] indicate different evaluation-behaviours." >}} and ‘Knowledge From Falsehood’: An agent is _gettiered_ if they encounter misleading evidence on which they (explicitly or implicitly) form a false belief q, and from which they infer a belief made accurate by some independent backup truth-maker. An agent can gain ‘Knowledge From Falsehood’ if the backup truth-maker is interdependent on the presence of the misleading evidence for q.

Gaining knowledge by {{< sc >}}Testimony{{< /sc >}} involves at least four points at which {{< sc >}}Truth From False Premise{{< /sc >}} can be introduced as an additional complicating factor:

- The attester’s evidential base for attesting that p can be based on a false premise.
- The receiver’s choice of attester can be based on a false premise.
- The transmission process can take place in a way so the receiver’s assumption that it was undisturbed amounts to a false premise.
- The receiver’s interpretation of the testimony can be based on a false premise.

In this preliminary taxonomy, I concentrated on identifying [case-types] where {{< sc >}}Truth From False Premise{{< /sc >}} manifests as _gettiering_ rather than ‘Knowledge From Falsehood’, i.e., where the backup truth-maker is independent of the presence of the misleading evidence. I do include [case-types] that involve the ‘Knowledge From Falsehood’ structure, for which I’m aware of relevant case-tokens in the literature on ‘Knowledge From Falsehood’ that needs to be demarcated form tokens of the first-level complexity [Knowledge From Falsehood]. Interestingly, I realised that when it comes to the receiver’s choice of attester, I was unable to implement a [case-type] where choosing a trustworthy and reliable attester based on false premises amounts to _gettiering_, at least not without a third complicating factor (see the comment on [Receiver Bases Trust in Reliable Attester on False Premise] for more details). Same applies for instances where S gains a false premise for a true belief that p by an [Unsuccessful Transmission] of testimony that p (see the comment on [Receiver of Unsuccessfully Transmitted Testimony Gains Knowledge From Falsehood]).
