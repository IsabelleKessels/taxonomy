The complicating factor of {{< sc >}}Help or Hindrance{{< /sc >}} affects an agent’s perceptual or cognitive belief-formation processes. {{< sc >}}Testimony{{< /sc >}} cases involve (at least) two agents, attester R and receiver S: either of whom can be _helped_ or _hindered_. Below I identify [case-types] which capture cases from the literature, alongside further obvious combinations of the two complicating factors. I consider the difference between the effects of _direct cognitive_ vs. _indirect cognitive_ vs. _sensory_ {{< sc >}}Help or Hindrance{{< /sc >}}: but limit myself to the variants of _help_ which result in agents’ gaining a true belief, and variants of _hindrance_ which result in the agents’ gaining a false belief. Consequently, the list does not represent all conceivable combinations of the two complicating factors. If and when further tokens find attention in the x-phi and armchair literature, respective [case-types] can (and should) be identified.
