For {{< sc >}}Epistemically Noteworthy Environments{{< /sc >}}, I suggested that this complicating factor can take two forms: environments can be noteworthy because they are _unfriendly_ or contain an _unexpected regularity_. When combined with {{< sc >}}Testimony{{< /sc >}}, an [Unfriendly Environment]{{< fn 1 "In this preliminary taxonomy, I don’t differentiate [case-types] in this complex {{< sc >}}Category{{< /sc >}} by whether the _unfriendly_ environment manifests as [Unfriendly Environment] or [Unfriendly Instruments Environment]. Separate [case-types] can (and should) be established _if_ x-phi results on tokens of the corresponding elementary [types] indicate different evaluation-behaviours." >}} can affect the attester’s grounds for attesting, or the receiver’s choice of attester. The transmission process, however, can’t be subject to the factor of unfriendly environments.{{< fn 2 " A case in which there are several transmission processes to choose from, and some of which would distort the message whereas others are reliable, and the process used in the @ world is reliable (i.e., non-distorting), has to be subsumed under C.3., [Counterfactually Distorting Transmission Process] or [Counterfactually Failed Transmission Process], because the distortion *does not occur* in the @-world; it involves the complication of {{< sc >}}Counterfactual-Interference.{{< /sc >}}" >}} The [case-types] below also includes ones in which the receiver’s choice of attester takes place in an environment featuring an _unexpected regularity_.
