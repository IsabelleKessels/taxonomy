---
title: A Preliminary Taxonomy of Thought Experiments on Knowledge
---

This website is a supplement to _Operationalising Theory-Construction_, where I propose a method for building taxonomies of thought experiments on philosophical concepts. Here, I put this method into practice for the concept _knowledge_ as both a tool for fellow epistemologists, and a demonstration of its utility in theory-construction and -testing.

Since (complex) thought experiments are so prevalent in epistemology, any individual epistemologist’s knowledge of cases inevitably includes blind spots. The best way to fill these in, and thereby progress to a more comprehensive taxonomy, lies in collaborative taxonomisation. The source material for this website is therefor available under an Open Access [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0) licence ![](/img/cc.svg)![](/img/by.svg). This format allows others to create their own working branches of the taxonomy, I warmly invite you to join me in this exercise of public philosophy.
